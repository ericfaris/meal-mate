The cooking isn’t the hard part. Deciding is.

Someone is standing in front of an open fridge. Practice ran late, everyone is hungry, and the same conversation starts again.

“What do you want for dinner?”“I don’t know. What do you want?”

It happens every night, and it usually lands on the same person. They’re the one who remembers what you had on Sunday, who knows the chicken needs to get used, and who notices you’re out of cumin halfway through the recipe.

The recipes

Spread across bookmarks, screenshots, a cookbook, and a stained index card in a drawer.

The plan

Lives in one person’s head, so nobody else can help with it.

The list

Written on the back of a receipt, missing whatever you’ll need on Thursday.

That work is invisible, and it happens every day. Meal Mate gives it one home, shares it with the whole household, and turns it into a ten-minute job on Sunday.

How it works

Collect, plan, shop. Then stop thinking about it.

Step one

Put every recipe in one place.

Bring in the recipes you already cook, however you have them. Meal Mate pulls out the ingredients, steps, and cook times so every recipe looks the same and is ready to plan with.

  • Paste a link from almost any recipe site. No more scrolling past the life story to find the ingredients.
  • Snap a photo of a cookbook page or a handwritten card, and Meal Mate reads it for you.
  • Type in your own for the dishes that never had a recipe at all.

Step two

Plan the whole week in about a minute.

Tell Meal Mate what this week looks like and tap Suggest. You get seven dinners drawn from your own recipes. Keep the ones you like and swap the ones you don’t, one day at a time.

  • No accidental repeats. Recent dinners sit out, up to ten days back.
  • Easy nights stay easy. Keep it quick favors the shortest prep and cook times, and Vegetarian only does what it says.
  • Real life fits in. Mark a night as Leftovers or Eating Out, or plan last week to keep track of what you actually ate.

Step three

Shop in aisle order, without the second trip.

One tap turns the week into a grocery list. Meal Mate adds up the same ingredient across recipes, sorts everything by section, and puts the sections in the order of the store you’re standing in.

  • Three recipes need onions? You see “3 yellow onions” once, and which dinners they’re for.
  • Your store, your route. Layouts for Aldi, Costco, Kroger, Meijer, Trader Joe’s, Walmart and more, or set up your own.
  • Staples remembered. Coffee, bananas, and paper towels go on with one tap, so they never get forgotten.

For the whole household

Everyone at the table, on the same page.

Invite your partner, the kids, or the roommates by sending a link. The plan and the list belong to the house, so the person who plans isn’t the only one who knows what’s for dinner.

One list everyone adds to

Whoever finishes the milk puts it on the list, and the list shows who added it, so nothing turns into a mystery item.

A recipe box you keep tidy

Household admins own the recipe collection and decide what gets added. Members can fix a typo or tweak a recipe they cook often.

Ideas from everyone

Found something on TikTok? Members send in a recipe link, and the admin approves it into the rotation or declines it with a note.

On the phone you already have

Meal Mate runs in the browser on iPhone, Android, or a laptop. Add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app. No app store needed.

Sign in your way

Use your Google account or an email and password. Your recipes and plans are private to you and the people you invite.

Plan forward or look back

Fill in next week, or log what you actually ate last week. Meal Mate counts how often each recipe makes the plan, so you can see the family favorites.
